Privacy Policy for RetroToon TV

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how RetroToon TV ("we", "us", "our") handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

RetroToon TV does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Your interactions with the application, including the cartoons you watch and your watch progress, are stored locally on your device using a local database. We do not track your activity, collect telemetry, or use analytical tools that identify individual users.

2. Use of Information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use, process, or share such data. The app operates entirely offline regarding user data, ensuring that your viewing history and preferences remain private to your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app fetches media content from the Internet Archive API. Please note that when you interact with content, you may be subject to the Internet Archive's own terms and privacy policies. We do not transmit your data to these services.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@retrotoontv.example
